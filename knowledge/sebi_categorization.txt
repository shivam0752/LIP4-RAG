SEBI Scheme Categorization – Mutual Fund Scheme Types

SEBI mandates that each AMC can offer only one scheme per category to avoid duplication. Below are relevant categories and their investment mandates:

Large Cap Fund:
- Must invest minimum 80% of assets in equity of large-cap companies
- Large-cap = Top 100 companies by full market capitalization (as defined by SEBI/AMFI list updated every 6 months)
- Example: Mirae Asset Large Cap Fund → Benchmark: Nifty 100 TRI

Flexi Cap Fund:
- Must invest minimum 65% of assets in equity and equity-related instruments
- No restriction on market capitalization – can invest across large, mid, and small cap
- Example: Mirae Asset Flexi Cap Fund → Benchmark: Nifty 500 TRI

Mid Cap Fund:
- Must invest minimum 65% of assets in equity of mid-cap companies
- Mid-cap = 101st to 250th company by full market capitalization
- Example: Mirae Asset Mid Cap Fund → Benchmark: Nifty Midcap 150 TRI

ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme):
- Must invest minimum 80% of assets in equity and equity-related instruments
- Mandatory 3-year lock-in; qualifies for Section 80C deduction
- Example: Mirae Asset ELSS Tax Saver Fund → Benchmark: Nifty 500 TRI

Liquid Fund:
- Invests only in debt and money market instruments with maturity up to 91 days
- Must NOT invest in risky instruments (no credit risk below AA-rated paper typically)
- Example: Mirae Asset Liquid Fund → Benchmark: Nifty Liquid Index A-I

AMFI publishes the updated list of large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap companies every 6 months (January and July).
