Mutual Fund General FAQs – AMFI

What is NAV (Net Asset Value)?
NAV is the per-unit market value of a mutual fund scheme. It is calculated at end of every business day as:
NAV = (Total Assets – Total Liabilities) / Number of Outstanding Units
NAV is published on the AMFI website (amfiindia.com) by 11 PM on each business day.

What is a Direct Plan vs Regular Plan?
- Direct Plan: Investor buys directly from the AMC without going through a distributor. Lower TER (no distribution commission). Higher returns over long term.
- Regular Plan: Bought through a distributor/advisor. Slightly higher TER due to distribution commission. Same portfolio as Direct Plan of the same fund.
For Mirae Asset funds, both Regular and Direct plans are available on the AMC website and through platforms like Groww, Zerodha, CAMS, etc.

What is Exit Load?
Exit load is a fee charged when an investor redeems (withdraws) mutual fund units before a specified period. It is deducted from the redemption amount.
For most Mirae Asset equity funds: 1% if redeemed within 1 year; NIL after 1 year.
For Mirae Asset Liquid Fund: Graded exit load for first 7 days; NIL from Day 8 onwards.
For Mirae Asset ELSS: NIL (lock-in prevents early redemption).

What is a Factsheet?
A monthly factsheet is a one-page summary published by the AMC containing:
- Fund objective and category
- Portfolio holdings (top 10 stocks/bonds)
- AUM (Assets Under Management)
- NAV as of month end
- Expense ratio (TER)
- Riskometer level
- Benchmark and performance (1Y, 3Y, 5Y returns)
- Fund manager details
Download Mirae Asset factsheets from: miraeassetmf.co.in → Research & Information → Factsheets

What is KIM/SID?
- KIM = Key Information Memorandum: Condensed version of the SID. Contains key facts about the scheme.
- SID = Scheme Information Document: Full legal document describing the scheme's investment objective, strategy, risks, fees, and procedures. Mandatory reading before investing.
Both are available for free download on the AMC website.

What is AUM?
Assets Under Management (AUM) = total market value of all assets managed by the fund. Larger AUM generally indicates investor trust but may also limit investment flexibility for mid/small-cap funds.
