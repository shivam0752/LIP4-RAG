SIP – Systematic Investment Plan

What is a SIP?
A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows investors to invest a fixed amount at regular intervals (monthly, quarterly) in a mutual fund scheme rather than investing a lump sum at one time.

Minimum SIP Amounts for Mirae Asset Schemes:
- Mirae Asset Large Cap Fund: ₹1,000 per month (Regular & Direct Plan)
- Mirae Asset Flexi Cap Fund: ₹1,000 per month (Regular & Direct Plan)
- Mirae Asset ELSS Tax Saver Fund: ₹500 per month (Regular & Direct Plan)
- Mirae Asset Mid Cap Fund: ₹1,000 per month (Regular & Direct Plan)
- Mirae Asset Liquid Fund: ₹1,000 per month (Regular & Direct Plan)

SIP Frequencies available: Monthly, Quarterly (varies by scheme; monthly is most common).

How SIP Works:
On each SIP date, the fixed amount is debited from your bank account and units are allotted at the prevailing NAV of that day.
SIPs benefit from rupee cost averaging – more units are purchased when NAV is low, fewer when NAV is high.

SIP for ELSS:
Each SIP instalment in an ELSS fund has its own 3-year lock-in from its date of allotment.

How to Start/Stop SIP:
SIPs can be started, paused, or cancelled through the AMC's online portal, CAMS (camsonline.com), KFintech, or through a registered distributor/investment platform.

No penalty for pausing SIP; however, if 3 consecutive instalments are missed, the SIP may be automatically cancelled as per AMC policy.
