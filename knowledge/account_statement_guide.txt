Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) – Download Guide

What is CAS?
The Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) is a single document that lists all mutual fund transactions across all AMCs for a given PAN. It is generated by CDSL or NSDL in coordination with AMFI.

How to Download CAS (AMFI-mandated):

Option 1: Via CAMS Website
1. Visit: https://www.camsonline.com/Investors/Statements/Consolidated-Account-Statement
2. Enter registered email address
3. Select period (monthly or specific date range)
4. Receive PDF on registered email; password = Date of Birth in DDMMYYYY format

Option 2: Via KFintech Website
1. Visit: https://mfs.kfintech.com/investor/General/ConsolidatedAccountStatement
2. Enter email, select date range
3. Receive CAS on email

Option 3: Via NSDL / CDSL
- CDSL: myeasi.cdsl.com
- NSDL: eservices.nsdl.com

CAS includes:
- Opening and closing balances of all folios
- All purchase, redemption, switch, dividend, and SIP transactions
- Current market value of holdings
- Tax information (cost basis, gains)

For Mirae Asset Fund Statements specifically:
- Visit miraeassetmf.co.in → Investor Services → Statements
- Or log in to CAMS portal with email linked to Mirae Asset folios

Detailed Account Statement (DAS) – for a single AMC:
Available at: https://www.camsonline.com/Investors/Statements/Detailed-Account-Statement
Covers transactions for CAMS-serviced AMCs only.
