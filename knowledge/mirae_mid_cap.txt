Mirae Asset Mid Cap Fund – Key Facts

Category: Mid Cap Fund (as per SEBI categorization – must invest min 65% in mid-cap companies, i.e., 101st–250th company by market cap)
Plan Types: Regular Plan and Direct Plan (Growth and IDCW options)
Fund Manager: Mirae Asset Investment Managers (India) Pvt. Ltd.

Expense Ratio (approximate, as of latest available data):
- Regular Plan (Growth): ~1.77% p.a.
- Direct Plan (Growth): ~0.67% p.a.
(TER is updated monthly; check amfiindia.com for the latest figure)

Exit Load:
- 1% if redeemed/switched within 1 year from the date of allotment
- NIL after 1 year from the date of allotment

Minimum Investment:
- Lump Sum: ₹5,000 (minimum); ₹1 (additional purchase)
- SIP: ₹1,000 per instalment

Benchmark: Nifty Midcap 150 TRI (Total Return Index)

Riskometer: Very High Risk
(Mid-cap funds are classified as Very High risk per SEBI norms due to higher volatility compared to large-cap funds)

Lock-in Period: None (not an ELSS fund; fully liquid after exit load period)

Fund Objective: To generate long-term capital appreciation from a portfolio predominantly investing in equity and equity related securities of mid cap companies.

Tax Treatment:
- LTCG (held > 1 year): 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25 lakh (as per Budget 2024)
- STCG (held ≤ 1 year): 20%

NAV: Updated at end of each business day; published on AMFI website (amfiindia.com)
