Mirae Asset ELSS Tax Saver Fund – Key Facts

Category: ELSS – Equity Linked Savings Scheme (Tax-saving equity fund under Section 80C)
Plan Types: Regular Plan and Direct Plan (Growth and IDCW options)
Fund Manager: Mirae Asset Investment Managers (India) Pvt. Ltd.

Expense Ratio (approximate, as of latest available data):
- Regular Plan (Growth): ~1.78% p.a.

Exit Load: NIL

Minimum Investment:
- Lump Sum: ₹500 (minimum); ₹500 (additional)
- SIP: ₹500 per instalment (lowest among Mirae Asset schemes)

Benchmark: Nifty 500 TRI (Total Return Index)

Riskometer: Very High Risk

Lock-in Period: 3 years (mandatory per SEBI ELSS regulations)

- Cannot redeem, switch, or pledge units during the lock-in

Tax Benefit:
- Investments up to ₹1.5 lakh per financial year qualify for deduction under Section 80C
- Post lock-in: LTCG above ₹1.25 lakh taxed at 12.5% (Budget 2024)

Fund Objective: To generate long-term capital appreciation from a diversified portfolio predominantly consisting of equity and equity related instruments, while offering tax benefits under Section 80C.

Investment Mandate: Must invest at least 80% in equity and equity related instruments (per SEBI categorization).
