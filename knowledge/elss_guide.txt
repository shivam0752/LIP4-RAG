ELSS – Equity Linked Savings Scheme

Lock-in Period:
ELSS funds have a mandatory lock-in period of 3 years from the date of each investment.
When investing via SIP, EACH instalment has its own independent 3-year lock-in from its allotment date.
This is the shortest lock-in among all Section 80C instruments (PPF has 15 years, NSC has 5 years, tax-saving FD has 5 years).

Tax Benefit:
Investments up to ₹1.5 lakh per financial year qualify for deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961.
After the lock-in, Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) above ₹1.25 lakh are taxed at 12.5% (effective FY 2024-25 onwards per Union Budget 2024).
