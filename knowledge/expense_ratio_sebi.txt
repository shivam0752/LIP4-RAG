Total Expense Ratio (TER) – SEBI Regulations

What is TER (Total Expense Ratio)?
The Total Expense Ratio (TER) is the annual fee charged by a mutual fund to manage the scheme's assets. It is expressed as a percentage of the fund's daily net assets (AUM). TER includes fund management fees, registrar/transfer agent fees, custodian fees, marketing expenses, and other operational costs.

SEBI TER Limits for Equity Funds (per SEBI circular; effective from 2018):
AUM Slab → Maximum TER (Regular Plan):
- First ₹500 crore: 2.25%
- ₹500 crore to ₹750 crore: 2.00%
- ₹750 crore to ₹2,000 crore: 1.75%
- ₹2,000 crore to ₹5,000 crore: 1.60%
- ₹5,000 crore to ₹10,000 crore: 1.50%
- ₹10,000 crore to ₹50,000 crore: 1.05% (plus incremental 0.05% for each ₹10,000 crore up to 1.05%)
- Above ₹50,000 crore: 0.80%
Direct plans must have a lower TER than regular plans (difference = distribution commission saved).

For Debt/Liquid Funds, TER limits are lower (typically 25 bps less than equity limits).

How TER Affects Returns:
TER is deducted from the fund's daily NAV. A fund with 1% TER effectively earns 1% less in returns than gross portfolio returns each year.
