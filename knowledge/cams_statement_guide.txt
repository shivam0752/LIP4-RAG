How to Download Capital Gains Statement from CAMS

CAMS (Computer Age Management Services) is the Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA) for several mutual fund AMCs including Mirae Asset.

Step-by-step guide to download Capital Gains Statement from CAMS:

1. Visit: https://www.camsonline.com
2. Navigate to: Investors → Statements → Capital Gain / Loss Statement
3. Enter your registered email address (the one linked to your mutual fund folio)
4. Select Financial Year (e.g., April 2024 to March 2025)
5. Select statement type: "Capital Gain Statement (Detailed)" or "Capital Gain Statement (Summary)"
6. Enter the OTP sent to your registered email/mobile
7. The statement will be emailed to your registered email address as a password-protected PDF
8. Password for PDF: Your PAN number in UPPERCASE (e.g., ABCDE1234F)

CAMS Services AMCs (includes Mirae Asset Mutual Fund):
CAMS is the RTA for many AMCs. Mirae Asset Mutual Fund is serviced by CAMS. So Mirae Asset fund transactions appear in CAMS statements.

For KFintech-serviced funds:
Visit: https://mfs.kfintech.com/investor/General/CapitalGain
(Similar process for AMCs serviced by KFintech such as SBI MF, HDFC MF)

For a Combined Statement (all AMCs):
Use the Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) available via:
- CAMS: https://www.camsonline.com/Investors/Statements/Consolidated-Account-Statement
- NSDL: https://www.ndl.gov.in
