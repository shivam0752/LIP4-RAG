SEBI Riskometer – Product Labelling in Mutual Funds

The SEBI Riskometer is a mandatory risk-labelling mechanism for all mutual fund schemes in India, introduced to help investors understand the risk level of a scheme at a glance.

Risk Levels (6 levels as per SEBI circular October 2020 and subsequent amendments):
1. Low – Principal at low risk (e.g., Overnight Funds, Liquid Funds with high-quality paper)
2. Low to Moderate – Principal at low to moderate risk (e.g., Liquid Funds, Ultra-Short Duration Funds)
3. Moderate – Principal at moderate risk (e.g., Short Duration Debt Funds, Balanced Funds)
4. Moderately High – Principal at moderately high risk (e.g., Dynamic Bond Funds, Aggressive Hybrid Funds)
5. High – Principal at high risk (e.g., Large & Mid Cap Funds, Multi Cap Funds)
6. Very High – Principal at very high risk (e.g., Mid Cap, Small Cap, Thematic, ELSS, Sectoral Funds)

Mirae Asset Scheme Risk Levels:
- Mirae Asset Large Cap Fund: Very High
- Mirae Asset Flexi Cap Fund: Very High
- Mirae Asset ELSS Tax Saver Fund: Very High
- Mirae Asset Mid Cap Fund: Very High
- Mirae Asset Liquid Fund: Low to Moderate

Riskometer Review:
AMCs must review and update the riskometer every month based on the portfolio's composition.
The updated riskometer is published in the fund's monthly factsheet available on the AMC website and AMFI.
