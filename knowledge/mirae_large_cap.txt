Mirae Asset Large Cap Fund – Key Facts

Category: Large Cap Fund (as per SEBI categorization – must invest min 80% in top 100 companies by market cap)
Plan Types: Regular Plan and Direct Plan (both Growth and IDCW options)
Fund Manager: Mirae Asset Investment Managers (India) Pvt. Ltd.

Expense Ratio (approximate, as of latest available data):
- Regular Plan (Growth): ~1.55% p.a.
- Direct Plan (Growth): ~0.59% p.a.

Exit Load:
- 1% if redeemed/switched within 1 year from the date of allotment
- NIL after 1 year from the date of allotment

- Lump Sum: ₹5,000 (minimum); ₹1 (additional purchase)
- SIP: ₹1,000 per instalment

Benchmark: Nifty 100 TRI (Total Return Index)

Riskometer: Very High Risk
(Equity funds investing in large-cap stocks carry Very High risk as per SEBI's product labelling guidelines)

Lock-in Period: None (not an ELSS fund; fully liquid after exit load period)

Fund Objective: To generate long-term capital appreciation from a portfolio predominantly investing in equity and equity related securities of large cap companies.

Tax Treatment:
- LTCG (held > 1 year): 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25 lakh (as per Budget 2024)
- STCG (held ≤ 1 year): 20%
