Mirae Asset Liquid Fund – Key Facts

Category: Liquid Fund (as per SEBI categorization – invests in debt and money market instruments with maturity up to 91 days)
Plan Types: Regular Plan and Direct Plan (Growth and Daily/Weekly/Monthly IDCW options)
Fund Manager: Mirae Asset Investment Managers (India) Pvt. Ltd.

Expense Ratio (approximate, as of latest available data):
- Regular Plan (Growth): ~0.25% p.a.
- Direct Plan (Growth): ~0.15% p.a.
(TER is updated monthly; check amfiindia.com for the latest figure)

Exit Load:
- Exit load is applicable for redemptions within 7 days as per a graded structure (SEBI mandate for liquid funds):
  Day 1: 0.0070%
  Day 2: 0.0065%
  Day 3: 0.0060%
  Day 4: 0.0055%
  Day 5: 0.0050%
  Day 6: 0.0045%
  Day 7 and beyond: NIL
- No exit load for redemptions on or after Day 7 from allotment date.

Minimum Investment:
- Lump Sum: ₹5,000 (minimum)
- SIP: ₹1,000 per instalment

Benchmark: Nifty Liquid Index A-I

Riskometer: Low to Moderate Risk
(Liquid funds invest in high-quality, short-duration instruments; SEBI classifies them as Low to Moderate risk)

Lock-in Period: None

Fund Objective: To provide reasonable returns with high liquidity by investing in a portfolio of debt and money market instruments with maturity up to 91 days.

Tax Treatment:
- Gains taxed as per investor's income tax slab (added to income) – no distinction between STCG/LTCG for debt funds as per Finance Act 2023 amendments.

NAV: Updated at end of each business day; Liquid fund NAV may also be updated for previous day for instant redemption purposes.
