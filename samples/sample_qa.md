### Q2. What is the ELSS lock-in period?

**A:** ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme) funds have a mandatory lock-in period
of **3 years** from the date of each investment/SIP instalment. Investments in
ELSS qualify for tax deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act up to
₹1.5 lakh per financial year. After the 3-year lock-in, units can be redeemed
freely.

**Source:** https://www.amfiindia.com/investor-corner/knowledge-center/elss.html

### Q8. What is the expense ratio limit set by SEBI for mutual funds?

**A:** SEBI regulates Total Expense Ratio (TER) caps for mutual funds — for equity
schemes, the maximum TER is **2.25%** for AUM up to ₹500 crore, declining in slabs
as AUM increases (as per SEBI's TER rationalization circular). Direct plans must
have a lower TER than regular plans by at least the distributor commission amount.

**Source:** https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/sep-2012/rationalization-of-total-expense-ratio-ter-charged-by-mutual-funds_23689.html
